IMPORTANT

Identification limitations

A hash's length or prefix can suggest a format, but cannot prove its origin. This tool only verifies Argon2id and bcrypt encoded hashes. MD5, SHA-1 and SHA-256 are not suitable as standalone password-storage algorithms.
