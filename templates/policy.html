GUIDANCE

Policy design notes

  • Favor long, unique passwords or passphrases.
  • Screen new passwords against common and compromised-password blocklists.
  • Do not rely on character-class rules as the only measure of strength.
  • Use MFA and rate limiting as additional controls.
  • Do not force arbitrary periodic changes unless there is evidence of compromise or a defined organizational requirement.
